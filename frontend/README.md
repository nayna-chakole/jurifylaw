## ⚡ Overview

JurifyLaw Frontend delivers a streamlined, modern experience for uploading legal documents, reviewing clause risks with visual gauges, and chatting with contracts in real time.

---

## 📱 Complete Page Index (12 Screens)

1. **Home Page** (`/`): Hero section, 4-column features, workflow steps, trust matrix, and dark footer.
2. **About Page** (`/about`): Mission, vision, core principles, audience breakdown, and performance stats.
3. **Login Page** (`/login`): Clean authentication card with password visibility toggle.
4. **Signup Page** (`/signup`): Account registration with integrated Terms & Conditions modal.
5. **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`): Navigation sidebar, recent documents, chat history, and quick upload card.
6. **Upload Document** (`/upload`): PDF/DOCX drag-and-drop uploader with 20 MB validation.
7. **Analyzing Progress** (`/upload` animated state): 4-step progress animation with live percentages.
8. **Analysis Result** (`/analysis/[id]`): Document summary, circular risk score gauge (72% High Risk), expandable clause breakdown, named entities table, and PDF report export.
9. **Chat with AI** (`/chat`): RAG conversational legal assistant with chat history and instant replies.
10. **Profile Page** (`/profile`): User account details, member timeline, and total documents counter.
11. **Documents History** (`/history`): Searchable audit ledger with risk badges and pagination.
12. **404 Page** (`/not-found`): Custom error page with signpost navigation.
